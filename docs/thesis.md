# Investment Thesis

Each run derives and saves a focused thesis from its input topic. For example, `AI agents for SMBs` evaluates SMB AI-agent fit, while `healthcare startup` evaluates healthcare fit. The common investment bar is seed-stage companies with a clearly evidenced workflow, direct topic fit, technical execution, credible public signals, and traceable market timing.

The score is 0-100:

- Workflow clarity: 25 points, based on distinct workflow signals in the public record.
- Topic fit: 20 points, based on coverage of the saved thesis and its planned queries.
- Technical depth: 20 points, based on founder technical-background signals.
- Freshness or traction signal: 20 points, based on HN traction or YC batch, team size, and source diversity.
- Why now: 15 points, only when a dedicated public market-timing source supports it.

The rubric is deterministic: an LLM cannot move a score after the evidence is saved. `Take a meeting` requires 75 points and `Watch` requires 50, with technical and signal support from at least two distinct evidence URLs; lower scores are `Pass`. A dimension without evidence scores zero. Every memo lists what would change the call.
