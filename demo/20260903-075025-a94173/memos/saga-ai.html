Product

Saga AI provides voice‑based AI agents that automate back‑office patient‑access workflows. The agents read referrals, faxes, and forms, then act on them directly in the EHR for tasks such as prior authorization, eligibility verification, intake, and record updates. The solution is marketed as HIPAA‑compliant, SOC 2 Type II certified, and includes a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

Team

Founded in 2024 by Jenica Zhong (Founder/CEO). Jenica previously co‑founded Tile and spent 4 years at Palantir as a PM on big‑data and AI products. She holds a math degree from Harvard and is a math Olympian. The company currently has 2 employees based in San Francisco.
