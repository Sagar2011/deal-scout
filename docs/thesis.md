# Investment Thesis

DealScout targets seed-stage B2B companies using AI agents to automate repeatable, high-volume workflows for small and medium-sized businesses.

The score is 0-100:

- Workflow clarity: 25 points
- SMB fit: 20 points
- Technical depth: 20 points
- Freshness or traction signal: 20 points
- Why now: 15 points

`Take a meeting` requires 75 points and two evidence records. `Watch` requires 50 points; lower scores are `Pass`. Every memo lists what would change the call.
