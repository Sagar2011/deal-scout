# Investment Thesis

Each run derives and saves a focused thesis from its input topic. For example, `AI agents for SMBs` evaluates SMB AI-agent fit, while `healthcare startup` evaluates healthcare fit. The common investment bar is seed-stage companies with a clearly evidenced workflow, direct topic fit, technical execution, credible public signals, and traceable market timing.

The score is 0-100:

- Workflow clarity: 25 points
- Topic fit: 20 points
- Technical depth: 20 points
- Freshness or traction signal: 20 points
- Why now: 15 points

`Take a meeting` requires 75 points and at least four evidence records with technical and traction support. `Watch` requires 50 points with the same evidence threshold; lower scores are `Pass`. A dimension without evidence scores zero. Every memo lists what would change the call.
